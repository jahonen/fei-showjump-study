STEM: An Athlete who retires from a jump-off is placed:
OPTION_A: Equal first with all qualifiers
OPTION_B: Equal last in the jump-off after all Athletes who completed the round
OPTION_C: According to their preliminary round placing only
OPTION_D: They receive no placing
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.3.1 states that an Athlete who retires, is Eliminated, or withdraws with permission from a jump-off is placed equal last in the jump-off after all Athletes who have completed the round.
