STEM: Under JRs Art 267.6, what does the exception for 'certain special Competitions' mean for Athletes unable to complete the first round?
OPTION_A: They receive a full prize regardless.
OPTION_B: They may still receive a prize despite not completing the first round.
OPTION_C: They are automatically Eliminated.
OPTION_D: They must restart the first round.
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 267.6 provides that Athletes unable to complete the first round will not receive any prize, except in certain special Competitions, meaning those special Competitions may allow a prize despite incomplete completion.
