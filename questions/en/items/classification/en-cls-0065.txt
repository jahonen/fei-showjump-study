STEM: What is required of prize winners regarding the prize-giving ceremony?
OPTION_A: They must sell the prizes back to the OC
OPTION_B: They must take part in the prize-giving ceremony, usually with the placed Horses
OPTION_C: Only the leading rider must attend
OPTION_D: Attendance is optional if the prize money exceeds CHF 1,000
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 267.2 states that prize winners must take part in the prize-giving ceremony and should do so with the placed Horses, though the Ground Jury may make exceptions for safety reasons.
