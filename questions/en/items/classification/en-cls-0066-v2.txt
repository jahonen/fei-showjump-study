STEM: A team withdraws from the second round of a Nations Cup. How is it placed under JRs Art 218.3.1?
OPTION_A: Equal last in the second round
OPTION_B: According to its score in the first round
OPTION_C: Not entitled to prize money but placed by first-round score
OPTION_D: Disqualified
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.3.1 states that for teams withdrawing from the second round of a Nations Cup, they are not entitled to prize money and are placed according to their score in the first round.
