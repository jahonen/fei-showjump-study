STEM: What is the general rule about rugs on Horses during the prize-giving ceremony?
OPTION_A: Rugs are mandatory
OPTION_B: Rugs may not be worn, except sponsor rugs presented by sponsors of Competitions
OPTION_C: Rugs are allowed only in cold weather
OPTION_D: Only wool rugs are permitted
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 267.4 states that unless agreed otherwise by the Ground Jury in special circumstances, rugs may not be worn by Horses during the prize-giving ceremony, with the exception of rugs presented by sponsors of Competitions.
