STEM: Under JRs Art 268.1, for which of the following are FEI Badges of Honour for Jumping awarded?
OPTION_A: Winning any CSI1* Competition
OPTION_B: Completing the first round of specified Championship Competitions without retiring or being Eliminated
OPTION_C: Serving as an FEI Official
OPTION_D: Owning a Horse that competes in a Grand Prix
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 268.1 states that FEI Badges of Honour for Jumping will be awarded to Athletes who complete the first round of specified Competitions (Olympic Games, World Championships, etc.) without retiring or being Eliminated, subject to Gold/Silver/Bronze thresholds.
