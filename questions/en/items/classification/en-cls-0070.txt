STEM: Under JRs Art 268.1, what are FEI Badges of Honour for Jumping awarded for?
OPTION_A: Winning a single CSI5* Competition
OPTION_B: Completing specified team and individual Championship first rounds without retiring or being Eliminated
OPTION_C: Perfect attendance at Events
OPTION_D: Service as an Official
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 268.1 states that FEI Badges of Honour for Jumping are awarded to Athletes who have completed the first round of a Nations Cup/Longines League of Nations™ Competition at a CSIO for Seniors, the team and/or individual Competition at the Olympic Games, and the World and Senior Continental team and/or individual Championships for Seniors, without retiring or being Eliminated.
