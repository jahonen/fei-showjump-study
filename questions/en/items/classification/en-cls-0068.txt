STEM: What happens to prizes won in a qualifying Competition if an Athlete declines to take part in the final Competition for which they qualified?
OPTION_A: They must return the prizes
OPTION_B: They retain the prizes they have won
OPTION_C: The prizes are forfeited to the OC
OPTION_D: The prizes are given to the next qualifier
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 267.7 states that prize winners of qualifying Competitions retain the prizes they have won even if they decline to take part in the final Competition for which they have qualified.
