STEM: Under JRs Art 237.1, the distance between obstacles in a Combination may be less than seven metres in which Competition type?
OPTION_A: Table A Grand Prix
OPTION_B: Speed and Handiness Competitions judged under Table C
OPTION_C: Nations Cup
OPTION_D: Puissance
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 237.1 provides an exception allowing distances of less than seven metres in Speed and Handiness Competitions judged under Table C and for permanent fixed obstacles.
