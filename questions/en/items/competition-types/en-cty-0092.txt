STEM: Which Competition type may include a Joker as a difficult optional obstacle?
OPTION_A: Puissance
OPTION_B: Accumulator Competition
OPTION_C: Nations Cup
OPTION_D: Six Bar
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 240.3 states that the Joker is a difficult optional obstacle that may only be used in an Accumulator Competition. Art 229 governs Accumulator Competitions.
