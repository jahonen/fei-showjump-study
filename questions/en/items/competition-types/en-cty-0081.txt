STEM: In a Table A Competition Not Against the Clock, what happens if Athletes have equal Penalties for any place?
OPTION_A: They always have a jump-off
OPTION_B: They share the prizes and there is no jump-off, unless the Schedule provides a jump-off for first place
OPTION_C: The faster round wins
OPTION_D: They are placed equal first
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 220.1.1.1 states that in Table A Not Against the Clock, Athletes with equality of Penalties for any place share the prizes and there is no jump-off.
