STEM: In a Six Bar Competition, after a Refusal or Run-out, where must the Athlete restart the course under JRs Art 230.3.4?
OPTION_A: At the first obstacle
OPTION_B: At the obstacle where the Fault was made
OPTION_C: At the last obstacle
OPTION_D: At the judge's discretion
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 230.3.4 states that in the event of a Refusal or Run-out, the Athlete must restart the course at the obstacle where the Fault was made.
