STEM: Under JRs Art 220.1.1.3, a Table A Not Against the Clock Competition may have a jump-off Against the Clock if the Schedule so provides. Other Athletes are placed according to:
OPTION_A: Their time in the jump-off
OPTION_B: Their Penalties in the first round
OPTION_C: Their draw position
OPTION_D: Their prize money
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 220.1.1.3 states that other Athletes are placed according to their Penalties in the first round, and equality of Penalties for any place other than first leads to shared prizes.
