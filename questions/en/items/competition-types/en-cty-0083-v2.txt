STEM: A Competition over Two Rounds may have two courses that are identical or different in which respects under JRs Art 221.1?
OPTION_A: Only in the number of obstacles
OPTION_B: Track, number of obstacles, or dimensions of obstacles
OPTION_C: Only in the speed
OPTION_D: They must be identical
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 221.1 provides that the two courses may be identical or different in track, number of obstacles, or dimensions of obstacles, but the speed must be the same.
