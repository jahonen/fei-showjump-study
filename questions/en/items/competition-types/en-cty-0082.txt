STEM: In a Table A Competition Against the Clock, which statements about ties and jump-offs are correct?
OPTION_A: Ties on Penalties for any place may be resolved by time without a jump-off
OPTION_B: Ties on Penalties for first place may be resolved by a jump-off Against the Clock judged under Table A
OPTION_C: Ties for first place may be resolved by a jump-off Against the Clock judged under Table C if specified and there are no Longines Ranking points
OPTION_D: Ties on Penalties and time for first place always lead to a further jump-off
CORRECT: A,B,C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 220.2.1 lists the formats for Competitions Against the Clock judged under Table A: time breaks ties without jump-off (Art 220.2.1.1); jump-off under Table A (Art 220.2.1.2); and jump-off under Table C without Longines Ranking points if specified in the Schedule (Art 220.2.1.3). Art 220.2.1.4 says there may be a further jump-off if Penalties and time are tied for first, not that there always is one.
