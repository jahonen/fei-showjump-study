STEM: In a Normal Competition in Two Phases, what happens to Athletes with Penalties in the first phase?
OPTION_A: They proceed to the second phase but carry Penalties forward
OPTION_B: They are halted after the last obstacle of the first phase or after crossing the first finishing line if the first-phase time allowed has been exceeded, and do not continue to the second phase
OPTION_C: They continue to the second phase but may not win
OPTION_D: They are Eliminated
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 222.1.3 states that Athletes with Penalties in the first phase are halted after jumping the last obstacle of the first phase or, if the time allowed for the first phase has been exceeded, after crossing the finishing line of the first phase; they must stop and may not continue to the second phase.
