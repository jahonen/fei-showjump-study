STEM: In a Puissance Competition, the initial round must comprise how many single obstacles?
OPTION_A: Two to four
OPTION_B: Four to six
OPTION_C: Six to eight
OPTION_D: Eight to ten
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 230.2.1 states that the initial round of a Puissance must comprise four to six single obstacles, of which at least one must be a vertical obstacle.
