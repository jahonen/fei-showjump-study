STEM: The default starting order in a jump-off is addressed in which article?
OPTION_A: Art 218.1.7
OPTION_B: Art 218.1.8
OPTION_C: Art 218.1.9
OPTION_D: Art 218.2.1
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.1.8 states that the starting order in the jump-off must remain the same as the starting order for the round preceding the jump-off unless specified otherwise.
