STEM: What is the default starting order in a jump-off?
OPTION_A: Reverse order of preliminary round placing
OPTION_B: The same as the starting order for the round preceding the jump-off
OPTION_C: Drawn by lot
OPTION_D: Fastest preliminary round goes first
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.1.8 states that the starting order in the jump-off must remain the same as the starting order for the round preceding the jump-off, except where specified otherwise in the Schedule or the JRs.
