STEM: Under normal circumstances, a jump-off must be held under the same rules and Table as the preliminary round(s). What exception is allowed?
OPTION_A: A Table A Competition's jump-off may be judged under Table C if specified in the Schedule
OPTION_B: The speed may be doubled
OPTION_C: The obstacles may be made of different materials
OPTION_D: The Athlete may change Horses
CORRECT: A
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.1.6 states that in principle a jump-off must take place under the same rules and Table as the preliminary round(s), but the jump-off of a Table A Competition may be judged under Table C if specified in the Schedule.
