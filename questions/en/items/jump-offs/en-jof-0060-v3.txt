STEM: JRs Art 218.2.4 permits leaving out elements of a Combination in a jump-off, but which elements of a treble or quadruple may NOT be omitted?
OPTION_A: The first element
OPTION_B: The last element
OPTION_C: The centre element(s)
OPTION_D: Any outer element
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.2.4 states that if the Combination obstacle is a treble or a quadruple, the centre element(s) may not be omitted.
