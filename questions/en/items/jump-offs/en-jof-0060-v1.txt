STEM: Which aspect of a Combination obstacle may NOT be changed during a jump-off under JRs Art 218.2.6?
OPTION_A: The height
OPTION_B: The spread of individual elements
OPTION_C: The distance between elements
OPTION_D: The colour
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.2.6 specifically prohibits altering the distance between the elements of a Combination obstacle during a jump-off.
