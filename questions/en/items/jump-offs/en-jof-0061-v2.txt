STEM: If a vertical from the previous round is jumped in the opposite direction in the jump-off, how is it counted under JRs Art 218.2.7.1?
OPTION_A: As no obstacle
OPTION_B: As one of the two additional obstacles allowed
OPTION_C: As a Combination
OPTION_D: It is not permitted
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.2.7.1 states that if an obstacle included in the previous round(s) is jumped from the opposite direction in the jump-off, it is considered as one of the two additional obstacles allowed.
