STEM: An Athlete withdraws from a jump-off without the permission of the Ground Jury. Under JRs Art 218.3.2, where are they placed?
OPTION_A: Equal first
OPTION_B: After those who withdrew with permission, retired, or were Eliminated
OPTION_C: Not placed
OPTION_D: Equal last but before those who retired
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.3.2 states that an Athlete who withdraws without permission is placed after Athletes having withdrawn with permission, retired, or been Eliminated in the jump-off.
