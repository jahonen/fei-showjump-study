STEM: If the Schedule is silent on jump-off starting order for a Longines Ranking Competition, what applies under JRs Art 218.1.9?
OPTION_A: Reverse order of times
OPTION_B: Same as the previous round
OPTION_C: Drawn by lot on the day
OPTION_D: Fastest goes last
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.1.9 states that if the Schedule does not specify, the starting order will be the same as in the previous round.
