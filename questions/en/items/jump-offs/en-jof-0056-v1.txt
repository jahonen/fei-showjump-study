STEM: Under JRs Art 218.1.2, if the Schedule does not specify whether a Competition has a jump-off, what is the default?
OPTION_A: There is a jump-off
OPTION_B: There is no jump-off
OPTION_C: The OC decides on the day
OPTION_D: The Ground Jury decides
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.1.2 provides that if the Schedule does not specify, the Competition will be deemed not to have a jump-off.
