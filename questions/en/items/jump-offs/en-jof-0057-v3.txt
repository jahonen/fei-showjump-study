STEM: Which of the following is NOT one of the circumstances in JRs Art 218.1.3 for holding a jump-off?
OPTION_A: Tie for first place after preliminary rounds
OPTION_B: Tie for podium places at Championships/Games
OPTION_C: More than one combination with zero Penalties in preliminary rounds
OPTION_D: Any tie on time alone without reference to Penalties
CORRECT: D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.1.3 lists ties on Penalties (first place or podium at Championships/Games) and multiple zero-Penalty rounds as grounds for a jump-off, not arbitrary ties on time alone.
