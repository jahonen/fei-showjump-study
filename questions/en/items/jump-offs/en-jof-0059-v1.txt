STEM: Under JRs Art 218.2.3, for counting the minimum number of jump-off obstacles, how are Combinations treated?
OPTION_A: Each element counts separately
OPTION_B: Combinations count as one obstacle
OPTION_C: They are not counted
OPTION_D: They count as two obstacles
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.2.3 expressly states that Combinations count as one obstacle for the purpose of the minimum obstacle count.
