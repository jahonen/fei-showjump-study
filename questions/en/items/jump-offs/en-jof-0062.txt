STEM: An Athlete retires from a jump-off with the permission of the Ground Jury. Where is the Athlete placed?
OPTION_A: Equal first with all other retirees
OPTION_B: Equal last in the jump-off after all Athletes who completed the round
OPTION_C: They retain their preliminary-round placing
OPTION_D: They are Eliminated from the Event
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 218.3.1 states that an Athlete who retires, is Eliminated, or withdraws with permission from a jump-off, second round, or winning round will be placed equal last in that round after all Athletes who have completed the round.
