STEM: If there is no Grand Prix, under JRs Art 203.2.1 the one-Horse-per-Athlete rule applies to which Competition?
OPTION_A: The first Competition
OPTION_B: The Competition with the highest prize money
OPTION_C: The Nations Cup
OPTION_D: The Accumulator
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 203.2.1 applies the one-Horse rule to the Grand Prix or, if there is no Grand Prix, the Competition with the highest prize money.
