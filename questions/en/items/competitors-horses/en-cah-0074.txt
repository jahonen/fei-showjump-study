STEM: At a CSI or CSIO, how many Horses may each Athlete ride in the Grand Prix or, if there is no Grand Prix, in the Competition with the highest prize money?
OPTION_A: One
OPTION_B: Two
OPTION_C: Three
OPTION_D: No limit
CORRECT: A
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 203.2.1 states that at CSIs and CSIOs, each Athlete may ride only one Horse in the Grand Prix or, if there is no Grand Prix, in the Competition with the highest prize money, except that Athletes may ride more than one Horse in a Derby.
