STEM: Before the year in which they reach their 18th birthday, Athletes may not compete in which of the following?
OPTION_A: Olympic Games
OPTION_B: A Grand Prix at a CSI3* to CSI5*
OPTION_C: A CSI1* Competition with obstacles up to 1.20 m
OPTION_D: A Longines League of Nations™ Competition
CORRECT: A,B,D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 205.1 lists Olympic Games, Continental/Regional Games, Grand Prix at CSI3*-5* and CSIO1*-5*, Nations Cup/Longines League of Nations™, FEI Jumping World Cup™, Power and Skill, Derby, and highest prize money Competitions as prohibited for Athletes before the year they turn 18. CSI1* Competitions with 1.20 m obstacles are not listed.
