STEM: Which article governs dress requirements when competing and during prize-giving ceremonies?
OPTION_A: Art 207.1
OPTION_B: Art 207.2

CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 207.2 governs dress. Art 207.2.1 requires Athletes to dress in accordance with Art 207.2 and GRs Art 135 when inspecting the course, competing, and during prize-giving ceremonies.
