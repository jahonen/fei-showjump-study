STEM: If Events of different Categories are organised on the same weekend, how many Horses per Athlete are allowed per Category under JRs Art 203.1.1?
OPTION_A: Two
OPTION_B: Three
OPTION_C: Four
OPTION_D: Unlimited
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 203.1.1 states that where Events of different Categories are organised on the same weekend, the number of Horses per Athlete must be limited to four per Category.
