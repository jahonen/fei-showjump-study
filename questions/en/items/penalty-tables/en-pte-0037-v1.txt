STEM: Under Table C, how many seconds are added for an obstacle knocked down in an outdoor Competition?
OPTION_A: 3 seconds
OPTION_B: 4 seconds
OPTION_C: 5 seconds
OPTION_D: 6 seconds
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 217.2.1 states that an obstacle knocked down or Water Jump Fault adds 4 seconds in outdoor Competitions under Table C (3 seconds in the second phase of a two-phase Competition or any jump-off under Table C).
