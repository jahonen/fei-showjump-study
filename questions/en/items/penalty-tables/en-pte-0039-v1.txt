STEM: An Athlete refuses obstacle 3 and later refuses obstacle 7 in the same round under Table A. How many Disobedience Penalties apply?
OPTION_A: 4 penalties (first only)
OPTION_B: 8 penalties (total)
OPTION_C: Elimination
OPTION_D: No Penalty for the second
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: Under Table A a first Disobedience incurs 4 Penalties and a second Disobedience in the same round results in Elimination (JRs Art 217.1.1).
