STEM: In a Table A Not Against the Clock Competition, ties on Penalties are normally resolved by:
OPTION_A: Time taken in the round
OPTION_B: A jump-off only
OPTION_C: Coin toss
OPTION_D: Sharing prizes
CORRECT: D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 220.1.1 provides format options for Competitions Not Against the Clock judged under Table A, including the option that Athletes with equality of Penalties for any place share the prizes and there is no jump-off; where a jump-off is provided for, Athletes not in the jump-off still share prizes for any place other than first.
