STEM: Under Table C, which statements about Penalties are correct?
OPTION_A: An obstacle knocked down adds seconds to the Athlete's time
OPTION_B: A first Disobedience with no knock-down adds no seconds
OPTION_C: A first Disobedience with a knock-down adds 6 seconds
OPTION_D: A Fall results in a 10-second addition
CORRECT: A,B,C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 217.2.1 states that under Table C an obstacle knocked down or Water Jump Fault adds seconds, a first Disobedience adds none, and a first Disobedience with a knock-down and/or displacing an obstacle adds 6 seconds. A Fall results in Elimination, not a time addition.
