STEM: What information must the official results of a Table A Competition provide?
OPTION_A: Only the total Penalties
OPTION_B: The total Penalties and the placing only

CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 217.1.2 requires the official results to provide the total number of Penalties and also separately specify the time Penalties and any other Penalties incurred during the round.
