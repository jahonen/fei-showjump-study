STEM: An Athlete wishes to do a training round in a Competition Against the Clock under Table A. What must they do?
OPTION_A: Pay an extra fee
OPTION_B: Inform the OC at the time of submission of declarations and start first
OPTION_C: Obtain permission from the Ground Jury after the first round
OPTION_D: Nothing; it is not allowed
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 217.3 states that Athletes wishing to do a training round in Competitions Against the Clock under Table A or C must inform the OC at the time of submission of declarations, and those wishing to do a training round will start first.
