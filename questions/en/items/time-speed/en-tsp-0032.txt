STEM: Under JRs Art 255.2, when is the clock normally restarted after an interruption?
OPTION_A: As soon as the bell rings
OPTION_B: When the Athlete reaches the place on the course where the clock was stopped
OPTION_C: When the Athlete jumps the next obstacle
OPTION_D: When the Athlete crosses the finishing line
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 255.2 states that the clock is restarted when the Athlete reaches the place on the course where it was stopped, except in a Disobedience with a knock-down to which JRs Art 256 applies.
