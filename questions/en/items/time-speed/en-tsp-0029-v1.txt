STEM: Under JRs Art 251.1.1, the time of the round may start even before the Athlete crosses the starting line if:
OPTION_A: The Athlete falls before the start
OPTION_B: The 45-second countdown expires before crossing the starting line in the correct direction
OPTION_C: The bell rings twice
OPTION_D: The Ground Jury waves a flag
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 251.1.1 provides that the time starts either when the Athlete crosses the starting line after the bell, or when the 45-second countdown expires, whichever occurs first.
