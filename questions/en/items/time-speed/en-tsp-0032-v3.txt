STEM: Under JRs Art 255.3, who may start and stop the clock?
OPTION_A: The timekeeper
OPTION_B: The Course Designer
OPTION_C: Any member of the Ground Jury
OPTION_D: Only the Judge in charge of the bell
CORRECT: D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 255.3 states that only the Judge in charge of the bell may start and stop the clock; the timekeeper may not be made responsible for this function.
