STEM: Under JRs Art 256, when is the clock restarted after an obstacle has been rebuilt following the specified Disobedience with a knock-down?
OPTION_A: When the bell is rung
OPTION_B: When the Athlete reaches the obstacle
OPTION_C: When the Horse leaves the ground at the obstacle where the Refusal occurred
OPTION_D: When the Horse lands after jumping the obstacle
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 256 states that the clock is restarted when the Horse leaves the ground at the obstacle where the Refusal occurred; if it occurred at the second or a subsequent part of a Combination, the clock restarts when the Horse leaves the ground at the first element.
