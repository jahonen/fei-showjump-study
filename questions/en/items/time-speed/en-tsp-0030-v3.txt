STEM: If the footing becomes bad, which parameter may the Ground Jury alter before the start of the first Athlete?

OPTION_B: The number of obstacles

EXPLANATION: JRs Art 258.2 states that if the condition of the footing becomes bad, the Ground Jury may alter the speed provided for in the Schedule before the start of the first Athlete of the Competition.
