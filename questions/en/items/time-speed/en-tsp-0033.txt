STEM: Under JRs Art 256, when is a six-second time correction added to the Athlete's time?
OPTION_A: Whenever an obstacle is knocked down
OPTION_B: When a Disobedience displaces or knocks down an obstacle or a relevant flag as specified in the article
OPTION_C: Whenever the Athlete requests the clock to be stopped
OPTION_D: After every Disobedience
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 256 adds a six-second time correction when, as a result of a Disobedience, an Athlete displaces or knocks down an obstacle or a flag in the circumstances specified in the article.
