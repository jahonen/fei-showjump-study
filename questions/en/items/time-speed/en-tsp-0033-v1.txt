STEM: A knock-down occurs as a result of a Disobedience and the obstacle is rebuilt. Under JRs Art 256, what time correction is applied?
OPTION_A: No time correction
OPTION_B: Six seconds are added to the time taken to complete the round
OPTION_C: The time is reset to zero
OPTION_D: The time taken to rebuild the obstacle is added
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 256 states that after the specified Disobedience with a knock-down, a time correction of six seconds is added to the time taken by the Athlete to complete the round.
