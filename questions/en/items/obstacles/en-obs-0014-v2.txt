STEM: A vertical placed over a Water Jump is judged as which type of obstacle?
OPTION_A: As a Water Jump
OPTION_B: As a vertical obstacle
OPTION_C: As a Liverpool
OPTION_D: As a spread obstacle
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 236.2 expressly states that the vertical obstacle placed over the water is judged as a vertical obstacle and not as a Water Jump.
