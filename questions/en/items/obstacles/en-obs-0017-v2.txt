STEM: A closed Combination is distinguished from a partially closed Combination by:

OPTION_C: The distance between elements
OPTION_D: The presence of a Water Jump
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 238.1 defines a completely closed Combination as one whose surrounding sides can only be surmounted by jumping, while JRs Art 238.2 defines a partially closed Combination as having one closed part and one open part.
