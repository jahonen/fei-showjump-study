STEM: In a Competition scheduled with a maximum height of 1.45 m or more, by how much may the Course Designer exceed the scheduled height?
OPTION_A: 1 cm
OPTION_B: 2 cm
OPTION_C: 3 cm
OPTION_D: 5 cm
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 233.2.2 allows the height of obstacles in Competitions with a scheduled maximum of 1.45 m or more to exceed the scheduled height by a maximum of 3 cm at the Course Designer's discretion.
