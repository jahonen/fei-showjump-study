STEM: When does a Fault at the Water Jump occur?
OPTION_A: The Horse's foot touches the lath and leaves an impression
OPTION_B: The fetlock joint leaves an impression on the lath
OPTION_C: The Horse touches the water with one or several feet
OPTION_D: The Horse strikes the brush on the take-off side
CORRECT: A,C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 236.1.3 provides that a Fault occurs when the foot or shoe touches the lath and leaves an impression, or when the Horse touches the water with one or several feet. The impression of the fetlock joint or boot does not constitute a Fault, and striking the brush is not a Fault under Art 236.1.4.
