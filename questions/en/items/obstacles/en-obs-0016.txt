STEM: After a Refusal or Run-out at a Combination, what must the Athlete do under normal rules?
OPTION_A: Re-jump only the element where the Fault occurred
OPTION_B: Re-jump all elements of the Combination
OPTION_C: Continue to the next obstacle
OPTION_D: Circle once and resume at the next element
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 237.3 states that when there is a Refusal or Run-out, the Athlete/Horse Combination must re-jump all of the elements of the Combination unless it is a closed or partially closed Combination or a Six Bar.
