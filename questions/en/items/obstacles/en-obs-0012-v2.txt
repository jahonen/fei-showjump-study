STEM: What construction requirement applies to the bottom of a Water Jump if made of concrete or hard material?
OPTION_A: It must be painted white
OPTION_B: It must be covered with a softer non-slip material
OPTION_C: It must remain exposed
OPTION_D: It must be at least 30 cm deep
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 236.1.1 requires the bottom of a Water Jump made of concrete or hard material to be covered with a softer non-slip material such as a coconut or rubber mat.
