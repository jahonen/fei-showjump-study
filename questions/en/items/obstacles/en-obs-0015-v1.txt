STEM: How is the distance between obstacles in a Combination measured under JRs Art 237.1?
OPTION_A: From the centre of one obstacle to the centre of the next
OPTION_B: From the base on the landing side to the base on the take-off side of the next obstacle
OPTION_C: From the front of the first obstacle to the front of the second
OPTION_D: From the highest point of each obstacle
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 237.1 states that the distance is measured from the base of the obstacle on the landing side to the base of the next obstacle on the take-off side.
