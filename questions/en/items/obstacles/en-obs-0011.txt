STEM: For a spread obstacle requiring only one effort, what is the maximum permitted spread?

OPTION_C: 2.00 m
OPTION_D: 2.20 m
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 233.1 states that spread obstacles must not exceed 2.00 m in spread except for triple bars, which may have a maximum spread of 2.20 m.
