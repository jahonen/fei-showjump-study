STEM: Under JRs Art 236.1.3, which of the following is NOT a Fault at the Water Jump?
OPTION_A: The Horse touches the water with a hind foot
OPTION_B: The fetlock joint leaves an impression on the lath
OPTION_C: The shoe leaves an impression on the lath
OPTION_D: A foot touches the water
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 236.1.3.1 expressly states that the impression of the fetlock joint or boot does not constitute a Fault.
