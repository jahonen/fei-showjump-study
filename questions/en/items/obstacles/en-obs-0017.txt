STEM: What is a closed Combination?
OPTION_A: A Combination with flags only at the first and last elements
OPTION_B: A Combination whose surrounding sides can only be surmounted by jumping
OPTION_C: A Combination whose elements are so close together that they form one effort
OPTION_D: A Combination that may not exceed two elements
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 238.1 defines a completely closed Combination as one whose surrounding sides can only be surmounted by jumping; examples include an in-and-out, sheep pen, square, or hexagonal obstacle.
