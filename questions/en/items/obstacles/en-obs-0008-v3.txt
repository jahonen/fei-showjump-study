STEM: Which statement about obstacle supports is correct under JRs Art 232.3?
OPTION_A: Cups must be fixed so poles cannot roll
OPTION_B: Cups must allow the pole to roll and must be 18-20 mm deep
OPTION_C: Safety cups are exempt from depth limits
OPTION_D: Cup depth is measured from the outside of the standard
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 232.3 requires the pole to be able to roll on its support and sets the cup depth at 18 mm minimum and 20 mm maximum.
