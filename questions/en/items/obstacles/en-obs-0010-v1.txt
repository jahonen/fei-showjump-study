STEM: A Competition Schedule lists a maximum obstacle height of 1.50 m. What is the tallest an obstacle may actually be built under JRs Art 233.2.2?
OPTION_A: 1.50 m
OPTION_B: 1.52 m
OPTION_C: 1.53 m
OPTION_D: 1.55 m
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 233.2.2 permits a maximum excess of 3 cm above the scheduled height when that height is 1.45 m or more; 1.50 m + 0.03 m = 1.53 m.
