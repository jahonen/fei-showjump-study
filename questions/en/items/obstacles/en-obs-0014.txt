STEM: If a Water Jump does not meet the requirements of Annex IV, what must be placed over the water?
OPTION_A: A spread obstacle
OPTION_B: A vertical obstacle of not more than 1.50 m
OPTION_C: A triple bar
OPTION_D: No obstacle; the water is removed
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 236.2 requires a vertical obstacle of not more than 1.50 m in height to be placed over the water if the Water Jump does not meet Annex IV requirements.
