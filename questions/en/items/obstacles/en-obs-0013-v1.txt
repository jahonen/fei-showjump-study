STEM: Which of the following would be a Fault at the Water Jump under JRs Art 236.1.3?
OPTION_A: The foot or shoe touches the lath and leaves an impression
OPTION_B: The fetlock joint leaves an impression on the lath
OPTION_C: The Horse touches the water with one foot
OPTION_D: The brush on the take-off side is displaced
CORRECT: A,C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 236.1.3.1 makes it a Fault when the foot or shoe of the Horse touches the lath and leaves an impression, and Art 236.1.3.2 makes it a Fault when the Horse touches the water with one or several feet. The impression of the fetlock joint or boot does not constitute a Fault, and displacing the brush is not a Fault under Art 236.1.4.
