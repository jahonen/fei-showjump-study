STEM: A normal spread obstacle is built with a spread of 2.10 m. Is this permitted under JRs Art 233.1?
OPTION_A: Yes, because any spread up to 2.20 m is allowed
OPTION_B: No, normal spread obstacles are limited to 2.00 m
OPTION_C: Yes, if the Course Designer approves
OPTION_D: No, the limit is 1.80 m
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 233.1 limits spread obstacles to 2.00 m in spread, except for triple bars which may reach 2.20 m.
