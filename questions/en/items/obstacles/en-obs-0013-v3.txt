STEM: Which actions count as Faults at the Water Jump under JRs Art 236.1.3?
OPTION_A: Touching the lath with the foot/shoe and leaving an impression
OPTION_B: Touching the water with one or several feet
OPTION_C: Displacing the brush
OPTION_D: Knocking down a flag
CORRECT: A,B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 236.1.3 lists only touching the lath and leaving an impression, or touching the water, as Faults. Displacing the brush (Art 236.1.4) and knocking down a flag are not Water Jump Faults in this context.
