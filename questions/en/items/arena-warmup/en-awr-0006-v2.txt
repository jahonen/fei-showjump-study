STEM: For Competitions with obstacle heights greater than 1.40 m, what is the Warm-Up Arena height limit under JRs Art 242.4.3?
OPTION_A: 1.50 m
OPTION_B: 1.60 m
OPTION_C: 1.65 m
OPTION_D: Same as the Competition maximum
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 242.4.3 states that if the obstacle height of the Competition is greater than 1.40 m, Warm-Up obstacles may not exceed 1.65 m in height and 1.80 m in width.
