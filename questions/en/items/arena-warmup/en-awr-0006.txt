STEM: In a Competition where the maximum obstacle height is 1.40 m or less, what is the maximum permitted height of practice obstacles in the Warm-Up Arena?
OPTION_A: 1.40 m
OPTION_B: 1.50 m
OPTION_C: 10 cm more than the Competition maximum, up to 1.50 m
OPTION_D: 1.65 m
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 242.4.3 provides that for Competitions (except Pony Events) where the maximum obstacle height is 1.40 m or less, Warm-Up obstacles may not exceed 10 cm more than the actual maximum height and width of the Competition obstacles.
