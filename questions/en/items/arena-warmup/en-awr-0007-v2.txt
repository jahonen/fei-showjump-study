STEM: Which of these is permitted in the Warm-Up Arena under JRs Art 242.4?
OPTION_A: Gymnastic/training exercises
OPTION_B: Combinations for any Competition
OPTION_C: A minimum of one vertical and one spread obstacle
OPTION_D: Walking Horses over elevated poles in cups
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: While JRs Art 242.4.12 prohibits gymnastic/training exercises and Art 242.4.13 generally prohibits Combinations, Art 242.4.2 requires at least one vertical and one spread obstacle in the Warm-Up Arena.
