STEM: How are Combination obstacles numbered on the course plan?
OPTION_A: Each element receives a separate consecutive number
OPTION_B: They bear only a single number, with letters such as 8A, 8B, 8C at each element
OPTION_C: Only the first element is numbered
OPTION_D: They are numbered with Roman numerals
CORRECT: B
