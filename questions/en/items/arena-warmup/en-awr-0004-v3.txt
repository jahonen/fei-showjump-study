STEM: Under JRs Art 241.3.3, which statement is true?
OPTION_A: Combinations have separate consecutive numbers
OPTION_B: Combinations have one number and elements are lettered
OPTION_C: Only the last element of a Combination is numbered
OPTION_D: Letters are not permitted on the course plan
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 241.3.3 states that Combination obstacles bear a single number, with distinguishing letters such as 8A, 8B, 8C added at each element.
