STEM: Which statement about the Competition Arena is correct under JRs Art 241.1.1?
OPTION_A: The arena need not be enclosed during course inspection
OPTION_B: The arena must be enclosed and entrances/exits physically closed while a Horse is in the arena during a Competition
OPTION_C: Stewards may leave gates open to speed up course resets
OPTION_D: The rule applies only to outdoor arenas
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 241.1.1 requires the Competition Arena to be enclosed and all entrances/exits to be physically closed while a Horse is in the arena during a Competition.
