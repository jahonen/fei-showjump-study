STEM: What does JRs Art 241.6.1.2 say about incidents before the Athlete crosses the starting line?
OPTION_A: Any Refusal before the start counts as the first Disobedience
OPTION_B: A Fall of Athlete and/or Horse after entering the arena prevents starting
OPTION_C: The 45-second countdown may not be interrupted
OPTION_D: Disobediences before crossing the start line are not penalised
CORRECT: B,D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 241.6.1.2 provides that Disobediences occurring between the signal to start and crossing the start line are not penalised, but a Fall after entering the arena up to crossing the start line prevents the Athlete from starting.
