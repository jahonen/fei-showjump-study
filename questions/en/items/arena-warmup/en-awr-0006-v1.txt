STEM: A Competition in progress has a maximum obstacle height of 1.35 m. Under JRs Art 242.4.3, what is the maximum Warm-Up obstacle height allowed?
OPTION_A: 1.35 m
OPTION_B: 1.40 m
OPTION_C: 1.45 m
OPTION_D: 1.65 m
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 242.4.3 allows Warm-Up obstacles to exceed the Competition maximum height by 10 cm; 1.35 m + 0.10 m = 1.45 m.
