STEM: Which article governs whether a Fall after the finish line incurs Elimination?
OPTION_A: Art 248.1
OPTION_B: Art 248.2
OPTION_C: Art 263.4.26
OPTION_D: Art 246.4
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 248.2.2.3 expressly states that a Fall after crossing the finish line does not incur Elimination, while Art 248.1 governs Falls during the round.
