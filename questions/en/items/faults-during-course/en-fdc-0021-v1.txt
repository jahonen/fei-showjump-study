STEM: Under JRs Art 246.1.4, a regular circle or group of circles is a Disobedience unless:
OPTION_A: It occurs in the warm-up arena
OPTION_B: It is circling for up to 45 seconds after a Run-out or Refusal to get into position
OPTION_C: The Athlete circles the last obstacle jumped
OPTION_D: It is done at the request of a Steward
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.1.4 states that circling for up to 45 seconds after a Run-out or Refusal to get into position to jump an obstacle is not a Disobedience.
