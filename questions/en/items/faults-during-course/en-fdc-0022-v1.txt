STEM: Under JRs Art 246.2.2, when does a stop in front of an obstacle become a Refusal?
OPTION_A: If the Horse steps back, even a single pace
OPTION_B: If the Horse lowers its head
OPTION_C: If the stop lasts more than 10 seconds
OPTION_D: If the Athlete uses the whip
CORRECT: A
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.2.2 provides that if the stop is prolonged or the Horse steps back, either voluntarily or not, even a single pace, it counts as a Refusal.
