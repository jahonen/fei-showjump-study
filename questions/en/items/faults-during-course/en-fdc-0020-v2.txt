STEM: Under JRs Art 245.1, a knock-down is penalised when at least one end of the faultable part no longer rests on any part of its support. This rule applies:
OPTION_A: Only during the time of the round
OPTION_B: Between the start and finish lines, except as extended for the last obstacle
OPTION_C: Only to vertical obstacles
OPTION_D: Only if the Athlete admits the mistake
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 245.2 states that knock-downs occur between the starting and finishing lines, with an exception for the last obstacle if it falls after crossing the finish line but before leaving the arena or the bell for the next Athlete.
