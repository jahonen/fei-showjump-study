STEM: A Horse jumps an obstacle between two red flags. How is this penalised?
OPTION_A: As a knock-down
OPTION_B: As a Run-out, and the obstacle must be jumped again correctly
OPTION_C: As a Refusal
OPTION_D: No Penalty
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.3.3 states that jumping an obstacle between two red flags or between two white flags means the obstacle has not been jumped correctly; the Combination is penalised as for a Run-out and must jump the obstacle again correctly.
