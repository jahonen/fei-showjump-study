STEM: Which of these IS penalised as a knock-down under JRs Art 245.1?
OPTION_A: Knocking over the filling of an obstacle
OPTION_B: Displacing a closed Combination
OPTION_C: Knocking down a flag while in the act of jumping
OPTION_D: Knocking down the whole or any upper part of an obstacle
CORRECT: D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 245.1.1 states that a knock-down occurs when, through a mistake of the Athlete/Horse Combination, the whole or any upper part of the same vertical plane of an obstacle falls. The cases in Art 245.5 do not incur knock-down Penalties, including knocking over the filling of an obstacle (245.5.4), displacing a closed Combination (which results in Elimination under Art 263.4.24), and knocking down a flag while in the act of jumping (245.5.2).
