STEM: Under JRs Art 249.2, assistance authorised by the Ground Jury under exceptional circumstances is:
OPTION_A: Unauthorised assistance
OPTION_B: Not considered unauthorised assistance
OPTION_C: Always a Disqualification
OPTION_D: Ignored
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 249.2 provides that, in certain exceptional cases, the Ground Jury may authorise the Athlete to enter the Competition Arena on foot or with the help of another person, without this being considered unauthorised assistance.
