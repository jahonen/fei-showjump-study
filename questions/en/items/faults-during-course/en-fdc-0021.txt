STEM: Which of the following are considered Disobediences under JRs Art 246.1?
OPTION_A: A Refusal
OPTION_B: A Run-out
OPTION_C: Circling once for 30 seconds to regain position after a Refusal
OPTION_D: A Resistance
CORRECT: A,B,D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.1 lists Refusal, Run-out, Resistance, and circling as Disobediences. However, circling for up to 45 seconds after a Run-out or Refusal to get into position is not a Disobedience.
