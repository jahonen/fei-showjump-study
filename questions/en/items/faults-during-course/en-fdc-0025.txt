STEM: Which acts are treated as deviations from the course under JRs Art 247.1?
OPTION_A: Omitting a compulsory turning point
OPTION_B: Jumping an obstacle out of order
OPTION_C: Jumping an obstacle in the wrong direction
OPTION_D: Circling once after a Refusal to regain position
CORRECT: A,B,C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 247.1 addresses deviations including omitting a compulsory turning point, jumping an obstacle out of order, and jumping in the wrong direction. Circling after a Refusal is governed by Art 246.1.4.
