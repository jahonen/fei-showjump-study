STEM: Under JRs Art 246.3.1, a Run-out occurs when the Horse:
OPTION_A: Stops in front of an obstacle and steps back
OPTION_B: Escapes the Athlete's control and avoids an obstacle it must jump
OPTION_C: Rears at the start line
OPTION_D: Jumps an obstacle between two red flags
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.3.1 defines a Run-out as the Horse escaping the control of its Athlete and avoiding an obstacle that it must jump or a compulsory turning point that it must pass.
