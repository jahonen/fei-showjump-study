STEM: Which article defines unauthorised assistance as any physical intervention by a third party during a round with the object of helping the Athlete or their Horse?
OPTION_A: Art 248
OPTION_B: Art 249.1
OPTION_C: Art 250.1
OPTION_D: Art 263.4
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 249.1 defines unauthorised assistance as any physical intervention by a third party between crossing the starting line in the correct direction and crossing the finishing line after jumping the last obstacle, whether solicited or not, with the object of helping the Athlete or their Horse. Art 249.2 contains exceptions.
