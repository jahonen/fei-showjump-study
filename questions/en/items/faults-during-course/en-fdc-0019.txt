STEM: Which of the following are Faults that normally incur Penalties during a round under JRs Art 244.1?
OPTION_A: Knocking down an obstacle
OPTION_B: A Fall of a Horse and/or Athlete
OPTION_C: Circling for up to 45 seconds after a Run-out or Refusal to get into position
OPTION_D: Exceeding the time allowed
CORRECT: A,B,D
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 244.1 lists knocking down an obstacle, a Fault at the Water Jump, a Disobedience, deviation from the course, a Fall, unauthorised assistance, and exceeding the time allowed or time limit as Faults incurring Penalties. Under Art 246.1.4, circling for up to 45 seconds after a Run-out or Refusal to get into position is not a Disobedience and therefore not a Fault under Art 244.1.3.
