STEM: If a Horse shows Resistance for 45 consecutive seconds during a round, what is the consequence?
OPTION_A: 4 penalties are added
OPTION_B: The Athlete receives a warning
OPTION_C: Elimination
OPTION_D: The clock is stopped but the round continues
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.4.3 provides that if there is Resistance from the Horse for 45 consecutive seconds, the Athlete/Horse Combination will be Eliminated.
