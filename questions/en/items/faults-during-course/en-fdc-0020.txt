STEM: A knock-down occurs when, through a mistake of the Athlete/Horse Combination, the whole or any upper part of the same vertical plane of an obstacle falls. What does this mean for a vertical obstacle made of two parts stacked in the same vertical plane?
OPTION_A: Only the fall of the top part is penalised
OPTION_B: Both parts must fall for a knock-down
OPTION_C: The lower part falling is always penalised
OPTION_D: Neither part falling counts as a knock-down
CORRECT: A
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 245.1.1 and the following paragraph state that when parts are placed one above the other in the same vertical plane, only the fall of the top part is penalised.
