STEM: Under JRs Art 246.3.3, if a Horse jumps between two white flags, the correct response is to:
OPTION_A: Continue to the next obstacle
OPTION_B: Retrace and jump correctly
OPTION_C: Stop for 45 seconds
OPTION_D: Accept the obstacle as jumped
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.3.3 treats jumping between two white flags or two red flags as a Run-out and requires the obstacle to be jumped again correctly.
