STEM: Which colour flags must be on the Athlete's right when passing an obstacle correctly?
OPTION_A: White
OPTION_B: Red
OPTION_C: Blue
OPTION_D: Yellow
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 241.5.3 requires the Athlete to pass between the flags with the red flag on their right and the white flag on their left. Jumping an obstacle between two flags of the same colour is penalised as a Run-out under Art 246.3.3.
