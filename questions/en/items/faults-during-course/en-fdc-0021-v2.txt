STEM: Which of these is NOT listed as a Disobedience in JRs Art 246.1?
OPTION_A: A Refusal
OPTION_B: A Run-out
OPTION_C: A Fall
OPTION_D: A Resistance
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 246.1 lists Refusal, Run-out, Resistance, and circling as Disobediences. A Fall is addressed separately in JRs Art 248 and is not a Disobedience.
