STEM: What generally constitutes unauthorised assistance during a round?
OPTION_A: A Ground Jury member signalling to stop
OPTION_B: Any physical intervention by a third party during a round with the object of helping the Athlete or their Horse
OPTION_C: The Athlete's coach shouting instructions from outside the arena
OPTION_D: Any assistance authorised by the Ground Jury
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 249.1 defines unauthorised assistance as any physical intervention by a third party between crossing the starting line and the finishing line after jumping the last obstacle, whether solicited or not, with the object of helping the Athlete or their Horse. Unauthorised assistance during a round results in Elimination.
