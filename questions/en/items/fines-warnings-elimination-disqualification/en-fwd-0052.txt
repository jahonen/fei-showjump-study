STEM: Which acts may constitute abuse of a Horse under JRs Art 265.1?
OPTION_A: Excessive use of the whip
OPTION_B: Use of a whip as a normal riding aid
OPTION_C: Rapping the Horse
OPTION_D: Touching the Horse gently with the spur to maintain straightness
CORRECT: A,C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 265.1.1 prohibits rapping, and Art 265.1.2 prohibits any excessive use of the whip. Use of a whip as a normal riding aid and normal use of spurs are not considered abuse.
