STEM: Use of a mobile phone while mounted in the Warm-Up Arena during a Competition under JRs Art 207.3.1 results in:
OPTION_A: Elimination
OPTION_B: A Warning
OPTION_C: A fine
OPTION_D: No consequence
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 207.3.1 states that mobile phone devices may never be used by an Athlete while mounted in the Warm-Up Arena during a Competition, and failure to comply incurs a Warning in accordance with Art 261.
