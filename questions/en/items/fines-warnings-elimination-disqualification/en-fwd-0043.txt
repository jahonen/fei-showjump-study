STEM: What is the general effect of Elimination in a Competition?
OPTION_A: The Athlete may continue but with 4 additional Penalties
OPTION_B: The Athlete with the Horse in question may not continue in the Competition at issue
OPTION_C: The Athlete is fined CHF 1,000
OPTION_D: The Athlete is placed last but retains eligibility for later Competitions
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 263.1 states that unless specified otherwise in the JRs, Elimination means that the Athlete with the Horse in question may not continue in the Competition at issue.
