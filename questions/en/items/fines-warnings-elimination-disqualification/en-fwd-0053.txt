STEM: Under JRs Art 266, boot and bandage control is mandatory for all Horses taking part in which Competitions?
OPTION_A: Only the Grand Prix
OPTION_B: The Grand Prix, the highest prize money Competition, Nations Cup/Longines League of Nations™, and Puissance and Six Bar Competitions
OPTION_C: Every Competition at the Event
OPTION_D: Only Puissance and Six Bar Competitions
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 266 provides that boot and bandage control is mandatory on all Horses taking part in the Grand Prix, the Competition with the highest prize money at each Event (if not the Grand Prix), Nations Cup/Longines League of Nations™, and Puissance and Six Bar Competitions, and recommended during other Competitions.
