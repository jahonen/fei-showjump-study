STEM: An Athlete uses a mobile phone device in the Competition Arena during a Competition. What is the consequence?
OPTION_A: A warning
OPTION_B: A fine of CHF 1,000
OPTION_C: Elimination
OPTION_D: No Penalty if the phone is in a pocket
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 263.4.30 states that an Athlete using a mobile phone device, other electronic communication device, or earphone(s) in the Competition Arena during a Competition results in Elimination.
