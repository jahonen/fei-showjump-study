STEM: Which article requires boot and bandage control to be carried out for Horses taking part in the Grand Prix, the highest prize money Competition, Nations Cup/Longines League of Nations™, and Puissance and Six Bar Competitions?
OPTION_A: Art 264
OPTION_B: Art 265
OPTION_C: Art 266
OPTION_D: Art 275
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 266 is titled 'Boot and Bandage Control' and provides that it is mandatory to carry out boot and bandage control on all Horses taking part in the Grand Prix, the highest prize money Competition at each Event (if not the Grand Prix), Nations Cup/Longines League of Nations™, and Puissance and Six Bar Competitions.
