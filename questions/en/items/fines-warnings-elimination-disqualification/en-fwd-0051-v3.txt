STEM: Which of the following is NOT a mandatory Disqualification under JRs Art 264.2?
OPTION_A: Marks of excessive spur/whip use
OPTION_B: Jumping unauthorised obstacles on the Event venue
OPTION_C: Failing to wear a jacket in warm weather
OPTION_D: Moving a Horse to unofficial stables without authorisation
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 264.2 lists mandatory Disqualifications including excessive spur/whip marks, jumping unauthorised obstacles, and moving a Horse to unofficial stables. A dress-code exception for warm weather is possible under Art 207.2.3.4.
