STEM: Under JRs Art 275.2, medication control of Horses at Jumping Events is conducted in accordance with which rules?
OPTION_A: The Jumping Rules only
OPTION_B: The GRs and VRs only
OPTION_C: The GRs, VRs, Equine Anti-Doping and Controlled Medication Regulations, and any other applicable FEI Rules or Regulations
OPTION_D: The Schedule of the Event only
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 275.2 states that medication control must be conducted in accordance with the GRs, VRs, Equine Anti-Doping and Controlled Medication Regulations, and any other applicable FEI Rules or Regulations.
