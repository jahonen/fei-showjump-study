STEM: Under JRs Art 275.1.3.1, if Horses cannot be present at the first Horse inspection due to exceptional, unforeseen circumstances, who may allow another Horse inspection at a later time?
OPTION_A: The Chef d'Equipe
OPTION_B: The OC
OPTION_C: The Ground Jury in consultation with the Veterinary Delegate
OPTION_D: The Technical Delegate alone
CORRECT: C
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 275.1.3.1 provides that the Ground Jury, in consultation with the Veterinary Delegate, may at its discretion allow another Horse inspection at a later time for Horses that could not be present due to exceptional, unforeseen circumstances.
