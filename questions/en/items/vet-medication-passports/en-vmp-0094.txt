STEM: Each Horse must be presented for Horse inspection in which bridle(s), and what equipment must be removed?
OPTION_A: Any bridle; rugs may remain
OPTION_B: A snaffle or double bridle; rugs, bandages, etc. must be removed
OPTION_C: Only a snaffle; no other restrictions
OPTION_D: A double bridle; boots must remain
CORRECT: B
EXPLANATION: JRs Art 275.1.3.3 states that each Horse must be presented in either a snaffle or double bridle, and any other harness or equipment (rugs, bandages, etc.) must be removed. No exception is allowed.
